Jonathan Harker’s Journal.

3 May. Bistritz.—Left Munich at 8:35 P.M., on 1st May, arriving at Vienna early next morning. I should have arrived at 6:46, but train was an hour late.

The impression I had was that we were leaving the West and entering the East. The most western of splendid bridges over the Danube carried us into traditions of Turkish rule.

Beyond the green swelling hills of the Mittel Land rose mighty slopes of forest up to the snowy peaks of the Carpathians themselves.

Every known superstition in the world is gathered into the horseshoe of the Carpathians.

I was not able to light on any map or work giving the exact locality of the Castle Dracula.

The women looked at me in a frightened sort of way.

One old woman placed a crucifix round my neck and said:
“For your mother’s sake.”

The driver was thin and very tall.
